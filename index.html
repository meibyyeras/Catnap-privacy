Catnap: Puzzle Game Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Catnap: Puzzle Game is a cozy puzzle game. This policy explains what information is used by the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

Catnap: Puzzle Game stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including completed levels, stars, hints, daily streaks, sound settings, music settings, and haptic settings.

Game Center

If you use Game Center features, Apple may process your Game Center account, player name, authentication status, leaderboard scores, and related Game Center information.

In-App Purchases

Catnap: Puzzle Game offers an optional 5-hint in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app receives purchase status so it can add purchased hints after a verified transaction.

Rewarded Ads

Catnap: Puzzle Game may show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect data such as device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, approximate location, and performance data to provide and measure ads.

Children

Catnap: Puzzle Game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.
